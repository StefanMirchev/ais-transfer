About AIS

A focused tool designed for clear, controlled invoice exchange between trading partners.

Built for everyday business use

AIS (Account Intelligence Stream) is a desktop application that helps Sellers deliver invoices to Purchasers and lets Purchasers collect them — without relying on email chains or shared drives.

Both sides maintain their own lists of trading partners. Invoices move through a secure channel with clear naming rules, size limits, and per-Seller space quotas.

  • Separate Seller and Purchaser lists
  • One-click send and receive
  • Scheduled automatic receiving
  • Short messages between parties
  • Load-limit visibility and enforcement

Key Features

Everything you need to manage invoice flow between your trading partners.

📋

Seller & Purchaser Lists

Maintain clear lists of the businesses you send invoices to and those that send invoices to you. Add, edit, or remove partners at any time.

📤

Send Invoices

Prepare Seller folders and upload new invoices in a single action. Only valid 10-digit PDF invoices and approved text files are accepted.

📥

Receive Invoices

Download new invoices from your Sellers into organised local folders, sorted by Seller and Purchaser pairs.

⏰

Automatic Receiving

Turn on scheduled downloads so AIS checks for new invoices at set times throughout the day — no manual clicks required.

💬

Messages

Leave short written notes for a Seller or Purchaser. A red marker on the Message button shows when a note is waiting to be read.

📊

Space & Load Limits

Each Seller has a defined space allocation. Check Load Limit, Consumption, and remaining free space before sending.

📖

Built-in User Manual

Open the full AIS User Manual (F1 help available throughout the application) for step-by-step guidance on every feature.

🔒

Clean & Secure Channel

Non-standard or oversized files are filtered out. Stale invoices older than 24 hours are cleaned automatically to keep the channel tidy.

How It Works

A straightforward workflow for both sides of the invoice exchange.

1

Set up your lists

Add your Sellers (businesses you invoice) and Purchasers (businesses that invoice you) in the dedicated list editors.

2

Place invoices

Put ready-to-send invoices into the correct local Seller/Purchaser folders using the required naming convention.

3

Send or Receive

Use “Send Invoices” to upload or “Receive Invoices” to download. Optional automatic receiving runs on a schedule.

4

Stay informed

Check space usage, exchange short messages, and move completed invoices into your own archive.
